# Exploring the Bitcoin cryptocurrency market

- **Goal:** measure how concentrated and how volatile the cryptocurrency market was at the end of 2017.
- **Data:** two CoinMarketCap snapshots, `coinmarketcap_06122017.csv` and `coinmarketcap_06012018.csv` (market cap, price, 24h and 7d changes).
- **Method:** pandas filtering and ranking, log-scale bar charts of market capitalisation and of daily/weekly price swings.

Notebook: [`bitcoin-cryptocurrency-market.ipynb`](bitcoin-cryptocurrency-market.ipynb) · based on a DataCamp guided project.
