# Clustering heart disease patients

- **Goal:** group anonymised heart-disease patients into clusters that could guide treatment choices.
- **Data:** `heart_disease_patients.csv` — clinical measurements (age, blood pressure, cholesterol, heart rate…) from the UCI heart disease dataset.
- **Method:** R — feature scaling, k-means and hierarchical clustering (`hclust`, dendrograms), cluster stability checks and ggplot2 visualisations.

Notebook: [`clustering-heart-disease-patients.ipynb`](clustering-heart-disease-patients.ipynb) (R kernel) · based on a DataCamp guided project.
